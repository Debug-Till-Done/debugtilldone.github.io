Privacy Policy

Debug Till Done LLC · Effective date: June 7, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the following iOS apps published by Debug Till Done LLC:

  • FitnUS Goals — monthly fitness goal tracking and sharing
  • BeSeenAtNight — safety beacon and flashlight
  • ElevationTracker — GPS altitude tracker for outdoor activities

What data each app accesses

FitnUS Goals

Apple Health (HealthKit)

FitnUS Goals requests read access to workout activity data (type, duration, and date) to calculate your progress toward monthly goals. This access is granted through iOS's HealthKit permission system. You are prompted to approve or deny access when you first open the app, and you can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

iCloud (CloudKit)

FitnUS Goals offers an optional sharing feature that lets you view another person's goal progress alongside your own. If you use this feature:

  • Your goal configuration and progress data is stored in a private CloudKit zone within your own iCloud account.
  • You choose who to share your data with. Each person's data remains in their own iCloud account — there is no shared pool of data.
  • Debug Till Done LLC has no access to your iCloud data at any point. All data transfer occurs directly between participants via Apple's CloudKit infrastructure and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you do not use the sharing feature, no data is written to iCloud.

BeSeenAtNight

Camera permission (torch/flashlight only)

BeSeenAtNight requests camera permission solely to access your device's hardware flashlight (torch). iOS routes torch access through the camera permission system. The app does not capture, record, process, or store any images or video at any time. If you deny camera permission, the flashlight feature will not be available.

All app settings are stored locally on your device using iOS's standard UserDefaults system and are never transmitted anywhere.

ElevationTracker

Location (GPS)

ElevationTracker requests access to your device's GPS to record altitude and track elevation changes during outdoor activities. Location data is used solely to calculate and display elevation information within the app. All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external server or third party.

You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ElevationTracker.

How your data is used

Data accessed by each app is used solely to provide that app's core functionality. All processing occurs on your device. No data is transmitted to Debug Till Done LLC or any external server.

Data we do not collect

None of the three apps collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports or usage analytics
  • Any data beyond what is displayed within the app

The apps have no backend servers and no user accounts. No data of any kind is sent to Debug Till Done LLC.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your data to any third party. None of the apps include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data remains on your device or in your personal iCloud account (FitnUS Goals only), you retain full control at all times.

To remove all app data, delete the app from your device. This removes all locally stored data.

For FitnUS Goals iCloud data: iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → FitnUS Goals → Delete Data.

To revoke HealthKit access: iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → FitnUS Goals.

To revoke location access: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ElevationTracker.

Children's privacy

None of the apps knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, the apps are appropriate for general audiences.

California residents

Because our apps do not collect, sell, or share personal information, there is no personal information subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). California residents have no additional actions to take.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the apps after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
